## Keeper Height

An image-plane measure is a legitimate explicit engineering definition to discuss in the report because the brief does not prescribe metres or a three-dimensional reconstruction. Its correctness and usefulness still require evidence. The current continuous signal is the torso midpoint, computed only from four actual confident shoulder/hip keypoints, projected through the connected camera transform. Signed clearance is measured from an observed pre-contact ankle reference line parallel to the visible goal baseline, divided by the median observed upright preparation body height. The projected goal-post direction supplies the upright direction for torso lean. Forward keeper motion can invalidate the fixed-depth ground interpretation, and that failure is stored rather than suppressed.

The new `observed_image_plane_height_candidate` is the maximum three-sample median in the connected observed lateral extension. It is an observed maximum and, when the first dive is incompletely observed, only a lower bound on the sampled image-plane signal over a potentially longer phase. Its low/mid/high labels use engineering thresholds of 0.35 and 0.75 preparation body heights with a 0.04 boundary margin. A label describes this observed signal; it does not classify the unobserved complete event peak or establish physical elevation. Even an uncensored landing candidate does not validate the ground-depth assumption. A signed value near or below zero must be read in the stated image reference coordinates.

The complete `ordinal_pose_height_candidate` requires a connected observed onset, extension and grounded-landing candidate. `strict_height_candidate` additionally requires the source-bound keeper-mask identity prefix and compatible mask/pose phase evidence. These fields remain separate from the observed signal. `physical_height_metres` remains null because no validated metric vertical reconstruction has been performed. Saying that metric height was not estimated is accurate; saying that the teacher mandated metric height or that an image proxy automatically completes the requirement is unsupported.

Suggested report wording: "We operationalise the observed keeper-height signal as camera-stabilised torso-midpoint clearance from a pre-contact image reference line, normalised by preparation body height. We preserve the continuous signal and its supporting source frames. Ordinal labels for this partial observation are separated from complete first-dive candidates. Missing landing evidence, perspective-depth changes, and segmentation identity failures are explicitly recorded; no value is presented as a calibrated height in metres."

## Ball Position

The last visible ball observation is useful and reproducible but does not by itself establish the course event's final ball position. A ball can leave the field of view, be occluded by the keeper or net, disappear during a camera cut, or move after an apparent save. The operational output should preserve the source-pixel position, source frame and time, actual track/detection identity, observation gaps, the reason observation stopped, visibility at the observation-window end, and whether the end of play was observed. A terminal-position candidate requires the same ball identity at the stated terminal event. Otherwise the final-position field remains unknown and the last-visible observation is marked censored.

Suggested report wording: "We report the ball's last visible source-pixel position and its observation-window end separately from the event's final position. The former is an observed endpoint of the available track. It is not automatically the ball's location when play ends. Camera cuts, occlusion, identity breaks, and an unobserved event end produce explicit censoring rather than a fabricated terminal position."

Recommended fields are `ball_last_visible_xy_source_px`, `ball_last_visible_frame`, `ball_track_id_candidate`, `ball_detection_id`, `observation_window_end_frame`, `observation_window_end_reason`, `ball_visibility_at_window_end`, `event_end_observed`, `ball_final_position_candidate`, `censored`, and `censor_reason`. Actual project fields can use their existing equivalent names, but the semantics should remain distinct.

## Reporting Coverage

Coverage should distinguish raw observations, supported candidates, complete-event measurements and independently verified labels. Unknown outputs and honest censoring improve scientific reliability, but they do not make an unavailable requested feature complete. The existing 39 events are development data after repeated diagnosis. Any future untouched evaluation must use new or previously uninspected events, preferably from different matches; the ten upstream user-review references are development references and cannot establish accuracy on all 39 events.
