# Fresh Foot V1 Evidence Diagnostic

This diagnostic reads actual source-bound neural outputs without the ten upstream foot references. It does not establish correct anatomical labels. The original normal-model V1 package contains 26 neural events, seven candidates and 32 unknowns. The subsequent selected normal/medium package contains 33 neural events, the same seven candidates and 32 unknowns. The old artifacts remain unchanged.

In the normal-model V1 package, the ten `insufficient_temporal_evidence` cases all have continuous motion observations: context windows contain 12 to 14 valid ankle-motion pairs, and mirror median errors are about 0.025 to 0.055 person-box heights. The reason is therefore not simply absent poses. Four cases have only two actual frame-matched ball/pose contact samples: Kane, Trippier, Eriksen and Fernandes. The remaining six have three. Across all 26 neural events, the normal/mirror contact-sample count distribution is 17 events with `(3,3)`, seven with `(2,2)`, one with `(2,1)` and one with `(1,2)`.

The original ball-distance window includes the first departing ball although the ball may already be far from both shoes. For Kane, frame 250 has left/right distances 0.106/0.185 body heights, but departure frame 252 has 1.262/1.239. For Trippier, frame 260 has 0.138/0.467 and departure frame 264 has 2.267/2.291. For Eriksen, frame 255 has 0.113/0.103 and departure frame 257 has 0.858/0.850. A two-point median mixes an actual near stationary observation with an already distant ball; reducing the minimum sample count would not solve the interpretation. The nearest stationary-ball ankle can be the support foot, so it is also not a valid replacement striking-foot label.

The original motion normalisation independently divides each frame by its changing detection-box height. This introduces a second possible source of apparent bilateral ankle motion during foreshortening. V2 therefore records a preparation scale held fixed over the motion windows, requires matching confident hip/knee/ankle chains in both actual neural views, and retains the local channel-switch guard without anatomical relabelling. Its preregistered method is [FRESH_FOOT_V2_PROTOCOL_2026-10-12.md](FRESH_FOOT_V2_PROTOCOL_2026-10-12.md).

The V2 CPU replay in `outputs/fresh_course_experiment/foot_v2_selected` produced eight motion candidates, five right and three left, with 31 unknowns and zero new neural inference. All 33 available neural caches reproduced their stored V1 temporal evidence before the new calculation. Eriksen and Ramos gained right-motion candidates; Badelj's previous right candidate became unknown under the bilateral chain guard and insufficient accepted follow-through pairs. The remaining six candidates retained their side, with no direct left/right candidate reversal. Eriksen's paired follow-through median ratio is 2.343 with four supporting pairs out of six; Ramos's paired context ratio is 1.337 with nine out of twelve. These are development motion hypotheses, not foot accuracy or verified shoe-ball contacts. No threshold was selected using a foot reference label.
