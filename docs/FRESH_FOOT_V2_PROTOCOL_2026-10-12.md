# Fresh Foot V2 CPU Replay Protocol

This protocol records the method before generating the V2 development outputs. It is an engineering iteration using previously inspected events, not a held-out validation protocol. No reviewer foot labels, player-name priors, camera-side guesses or outcome labels are inputs. V1 artifacts and their neural predictions remain unchanged.

The V1 source diagnosis identified two mechanisms. Its ankle coordinates were divided by each frame's changing person-box height, which can introduce apparent motion during foreshortening. Its ball-distance summary included the first departing ball, although that observation can already be more than one body height away from either shoe. Neither issue establishes the correct striking foot for an individual event.

V2 uses one preparation scale, the median normal/mirrored source box height before the stationary/departure bracket. Each frame requires confident bilateral hip, knee and ankle joints in both neural views, with corresponding source-coordinate leg joints agreeing within 0.15 preparation body heights. The two actual observations are averaged only after this check. The original temporal channel ambiguity guard remains active in the local contact window; V2 performs no anatomical channel permutation or Hungarian repair.

Two fixed motion windows are used: the original bracket context from 0.13 seconds before the last stationary sample to 0.20 seconds after the first departure, and the departure/follow-through interval. Hip-relative ankle speed is measured using the constant preparation scale. A median ratio of at least 1.25, at least 0.65 consistent pair support and at least four connected pairs are required. The independently computed normal and mirrored median directions must agree with the paired estimate. Conflicting supported windows remain unknown. Knee motion is retained as a leg-chain diagnostic rather than requiring an arbitrary knee-speed threshold that could suppress a genuine ankle swing.

Actual stationary detections at or before the last-stationary frame are the only ball observations used for ankle-distance diagnostics. Their source frame and detection ID are preserved. Departure observations are solely the upstream causal timing gate. A close stationary-ball ankle, an approaching ankle, or a nearest ankle never determines the label because it can be the support foot. A motion candidate can be reported when only one local stationary sample is available, but it is explicitly not verified shoe-ball contact.

The replay checks the exact source-video, source-manifest, pose and contact bytes against the V1 neural producer, checks the canonical contact through the existing source-bound ball guard, and reproduces the V1 evidence from the cached cropped poses before computing V2. The new output records all input hashes, producer receipts, the preparation scale, accepted/rejected paired source frames, per-window pair speeds and stationary-only distances. It performs zero neural inference. Coverage is reported separately from accuracy, which remains unvalidated.
