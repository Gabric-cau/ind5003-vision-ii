# Fresh Foot V3 Stationary-Spot Diagnostic

V3 is a source-only CPU diagnostic over the cached high-resolution normal and mirrored pose predictions. It is preregistered here after the V2 replay and before interpreting the V3 outputs. It is a development experiment over previously inspected course events, not a held-out validation set.

The canonical ball search supplies actual stationary source-pixel detections and two actual departure detections. V3 keeps the last stationary detection as a fixed counterfactual spot. Departure observations only establish the causal follow-through interval; their positions are never used as an ankle-distance target. A stationary ankle distance is therefore a diagnostic, not an automatic striking-foot label.

At least three stationary detections in the final 0.15 seconds before the bracket are required, ending at the recorded last-stationary frame. Each stationary frame and at least two post-departure pose frames must have confident hip, knee and ankle joints in both source-coordinate neural views. The local normal/mirror channel ambiguity guard remains active and no anatomical permutation is performed. The camera transform is optional at the API boundary but, when supplied, must project the same source points and stationary target through finite exact-frame transforms.

For each image channel, V3 measures the stationary-spot distance reduction, the fraction of non-increasing approach steps, and the final stationary distance. After departure it measures ankle pass-through displacement relative to the last stationary frame and compares it with alternate-leg displacement. A unique side requires a reduction of at least 0.03 preparation body heights, at least 0.67 approach support, final distance at most 0.35, pass-through at least 0.10, alternate-leg motion at most 0.12, and a pass-through/support ratio of at least 1.25. These are pre-registered engineering conditions, not thresholds fitted to foot labels. Both normal and mirrored views must select the same side. If both sides fit, if either view disagrees, or if channel continuity is ambiguous, the result is unknown.

The outcome is a kinematic candidate only. It does not verify shoe-ball contact, anatomical left/right identity or the physical foot. The replay preserves the stationary/departure detection IDs, source hashes, cached neural producer receipt, paired-frame acceptance and all side metrics. It performs no neural inference and reads no reference labels.
