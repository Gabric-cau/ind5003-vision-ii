## Changes And Remaining Semantics

- The 2026-10-27 review already corrected match_09 to a miss. Its new contact
  time differs by only 0.01 seconds (10.45 to 10.44). Do not count that outcome
  correction as new or restore the obsolete positive value from old_labels.
- The new match_09 miss-reason category says "other", while its text and the
  previous review say wide. Quarantine that field, not the entire event.
- The raw snapshot clarifies core_07 pre-movement as screen-left. It is absent
  from the 24-line training JSONL and needs a separately versioned review
  entry. Its screenshot context time is not an exact movement-onset label.
- Relative to the original three-match table, match_20 changes kicking foot
  from right to left and match_22 from left to right. Preserve the new human
  observations and their provenance while checking the source frames.
- Eleven handwritten run-up totals differ from the old table. Start point,
  adjustment steps, and inclusion of the final support step are not fully
  specified, so differing counts do not establish model regression. The
  package correctly masks match_02's count because its run-up is incomplete.
- Low/middle/high reference boundaries remain unspecified. Retain the raw
  labels without inventing a geometric threshold.
- All 14 annotated records omit camera start/end fields, even though ball
  direction is populated in 14 and dive direction in 13. The original
  release shot timeline maps all 14 contact times to a unique shot with a
  camera label. Those existing intervals can support targeted review; do not
  ask for all directions again or silently unmask them. A contact shot alone
  does not prove which view a later direction annotation describes.
- Six moving events (03, 04, 08, 10, 20, 24) record an earliest-moving time but
  lack the last-definitely-stationary endpoint. Presence classification is
  possible; exact onset regression and complete-interval scoring are not.
- Support-foot candidates 03 and 04 rely on historical steps after contact.
  Isolate those timing claims before using them as contact-time support-foot
  supervision. Inferred opposite feet are not independent support labels.

## Evidence And Evaluation

The evidence index has 80 references, including core_07: 42 pasted-image
entries (39 unique images), plus 38 video-frame entries in five sequences.
The original annotation-player asset folder and these screenshot files were
not located in Downloads, Desktop, or Documents. The archive's exists flags
refer to its producer's machine, not to this audit machine. Existing source
videos remain available, so this does not block all work.

Pasted-image context times have imageTimeVerified=false. They cannot be used
as exact image timestamps. The new package has no video hashes; matching its
event metadata to a byte-verified old video is an association, not proof that
the annotation player's copy is byte-identical. Contact supervision needs
source-frame alignment checks.

Three source videos were subsequently checked with seven consecutive frames
each, spanning +/-0.06 seconds around the supplied contact times. Original
frames, enlarged contact sheets, decoded PTS, and machine-assisted observations
are saved in `outputs/training_preparation_audit_2026-10-29/visual_review/`.
The images support left foot for match_20 and right foot for match_22/24.
Approximate visible contact intervals are 17.76-17.78, 7.54-7.56, and
4.16-4.18 seconds respectively. Match_24's 4.20-second label is already in
follow-through, approximately one or two frames late at 50 FPS. These are
limited machine-assisted observations, not independent human verification or
permission to silently overwrite the source labels. The other contact times
were not visually validated by this three-event check.

The proposed three-fold split keeps match groups separate, with validation
sizes 8, 3, and 3. These fixtures have already been inspected and used for
development elsewhere; a newly written split does not restore independence.
The World Cup fold contains only positive goal outcomes among its three
populated records. Neither a small-fold score nor a single module's accuracy
establishes 90% performance on the full assignment.

This package is useful for event-target updates and contact supervision. It
does not add ball boxes, keeper boxes, or joint coordinates. Separately, the
older keeper package already supplies 835 training boxes and matching source
videos; the earlier claim that those boxes were missing has been corrected in
`CONTROLLED_MODULE_TRAINING_2026-10-28.md`.
